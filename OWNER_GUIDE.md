# Owner guide

## Process orders

Open **Orders** and select **Needs action**. Review the customer, fulfillment, items, and payment method before using the single available next action.

- **GCash:** open the proof and compare the amount and reference. Approve only after confirming the payment. If it does not match, reject it with a clear reason so the customer can submit corrected proof.
- **Cash on delivery:** confirm the cash order, prepare it, mark it ready/with the delivery person, then complete it after handoff and payment.
- **Pickup cash:** confirm the order, prepare it, mark it ready, then complete it when the customer collects and pays.
- Use the packing checklist and packing slip where available. Confirm that stock changes and order history are correct before moving to the next order.

## Products and inventory

Use **Products** to add or edit catalog items. In **Inventory**, choose a product and record a positive restock or a reasoned adjustment. Check the movement history after saving; do not make manual database stock edits.

## Settings and reports

In **Settings**, update the delivery fee and GCash recipient name/number, then upload the shop QR. Verify the public checkout displays the new values. Use **Reports** to select Week, Month, or Year; review the period before exporting CSV or printing.

## Notifications and problems

Open the bell or **Notifications** to review account and order updates. If an order, proof, stock figure, payment setting, or report looks wrong, stop processing it and contact the site administrator. Do not share passwords, reset links, private configuration, or customer data in public messages.
